Noam Goldgraber

About me

I am a PhD student in theoretical computer science at Tel Aviv University and Ben-Gurion University, jointly advised by Professor Gil Cohen and Professor Dean Doron.

My research focuses on pseudorandomness and coding theory, with a particular interest in applications of algebraic geometry to these areas.

Previously, I was an MSc student in mathematics at Tel Aviv University, where I worked on number theory and probabilistic Galois theory under the supervision of Professor Lior Bary-Soroker.
